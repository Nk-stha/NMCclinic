Before Your Visit

Helpful Information for Patients

A few simple details can help you prepare for a smoother clinic visit.

What to Bring

Bring a valid ID, previous reports, prescriptions, and a list of current medicines.

Opening Hours

Check the clinic schedule before travelling, especially during public holidays.

Test Preparation

Some tests may require fasting or other preparation. Contact the clinic when unsure.
