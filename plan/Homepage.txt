  Notes on current state:

  - The page has four placeholder doctor records at NMC Homepage.dc.html:710, using Dr. [Name], Qualification to be
    confirmed, and NMC Reg. No. to be confirmed. The Doctors page has six of the same placeholders.
  - blogPosts at NMC Homepage.dc.html:717 builds six posts and returns them from renderVals(), but no template tag
    reads it. The health articles section instead hardcodes three different card titles directly in the markup at
    lines 481-535. Two sources of blog content exist on one page and they do not match.
  - The appointment form at line 372 only calls preventDefault() and sets submitted: true at line 767. It does not
    submit to a backend, send email, store a request, or notify staff.
  - The testimonial section contains no testimonial. It reads: Verified patient experiences will be published here
    with permission.
  - The location section embeds the same OpenStreetMap coordinates as the Contact page (27.700, 83.448).
  - Opening hours appear twice on this page as hardcoded text: once in the header contact bar and once in the
    patient information section. They are not a prop.
  - The footer on this page carries an extra contact strip (emergency hotline, email, address) that the other six
    pages do not have. Homepage footer is 84 lines (594-677); every other footer is 58.

  Static Fields
  Keep these static unless redesigning: section order logic, grid and flex structure, card layout, filter tab
  behavior, sticky header behavior, hero overlay gradient, color tokens, spacing scale, typography, responsive
  breakpoint at 1180px, hover states, and icon rendering.

  Do not store raw card HTML or raw inline CSS in the CMS.

  Recommended Database Design
  The Homepage is a composition page. It should own almost no content of its own. Nearly every section on it shows
  data that already belongs to another entity: services come from specialties, doctors from doctors, articles from
  blog_posts, contact and map from global settings.

  Reuse these tables as already defined in the other plans. Do not redefine them here:

  - pages, seo_metadata, media_assets, page_sections, section_items  (aboutpage.txt)
  - specialties, specialty_points                                     (Specialties.txt)
  - doctors, doctor_specialties, doctor_schedules                     (Doctors.txt)
  - blog_posts, blog_categories, authors                              (Blogs.txt)
  - business_contact_settings, opening_hours                          (Contact.txt)

  Note: those five plans define pages, seo_metadata and media_assets with different column names. That conflict
  must be settled once before any of this is built. This plan does not add a sixth version.

  Homepage-specific rows:

  pages
  - one row with page_type home, slug home

  page_sections
  - one row per visible section listed in Source Truth
  - section_type enum for this page: hero, quick_actions, service_grid, about_teaser, diagnostics,
    doctor_teaser, feature_grid, appointment, patient_info, testimonials, article_teaser, location
  - selection rules for which services/doctors/articles to show go in settings_json, not raw HTML

  section_items
  - hero highlight items, quick action cards, why-choose cards, patient info cards
  - these are presentation copy that exists only on the Homepage, so section_items is the right home for them

  One new table is needed. Nothing in the existing five plans stores an appointment request:

  appointment_requests
  - id
  - full_name
  - phone
  - specialty_id nullable
  - preferred_date nullable
  - preferred_time_slot nullable
  - message nullable
  - consent_accepted boolean
  - consent_at
  - status enum: new, contacted, scheduled, closed
  - source_page
  - ip_hash
  - user_agent
  - created_at
  - responded_at
  - deleted_at

  Indexes: appointment_requests(status, created_at), appointment_requests(specialty_id),
  page_sections(page_id, sort_order, is_visible), section_items(section_id, sort_order, is_visible).

  Two tables are deliberately NOT added:

  - appointment_services. The form's six service options are the same six specialties. Read from specialties.
  - testimonials. The section is an empty placeholder today. Add the table when the clinic has real, consented
    testimonials to publish. Until then, keep the section hidden.

  ERD Recommendation
  Core relationship:

  pages 1--1 seo_metadata
  pages 1--many page_sections
  page_sections 1--many section_items
  media_assets 1--many page_sections
  specialties 1--many appointment_requests
  page_sections many--many specialties       via settings_json selection
  page_sections many--many doctors           via settings_json selection
  page_sections many--many blog_posts        via settings_json selection

  Future relationship:

  appointment_requests many--1 doctors
  testimonials many--1 doctors
  testimonials many--1 specialties

  SEO Architecture
  Recommended final URL: / , not NMC%20Homepage.dc.html. The current filename contains an encoded space, which is
  poor as a public URL and is linked from every other page.

  This is the most SEO-important page on the site. It should carry the clinic's primary identity.

  Recommended structured data:

  - MedicalClinic or MedicalBusiness with name, address, phone, geo coordinates, and opening hours.
  - Organization identity if it is not already declared sitewide.
  - WebSite for the site name.
  - Do not add BreadcrumbList; this is the root page.
  - Do not repeat the full clinic schema on every page. Declare it here and reference it elsewhere.

  Heading strategy:

  - One H1: the hero headline. Current page already has exactly one H1.
  - H2 for each major section. Current page has 10.
  - H3 for cards, service names, doctor names, and article titles. Current page has 18.

  Internal links: the Homepage already links to all six other pages from the header and footer. Keep the service
  cards linking to /specialties anchors, doctor cards to /doctors, and article cards to /blog/{post-slug}. Today
  the article cards link to # and the service cards link to the Specialties page anchors.

  Required before this form is made real:

  - A server endpoint that writes to appointment_requests.
  - Server-side validation of name, phone, date, and time slot. Client validation is not enough.
  - Rate limiting and spam protection.
  - Store the consent timestamp, not just the boolean.
  - Staff notification, and a visible response-time promise the clinic can meet.
  - Reject dates in the past and outside opening_hours.
  - The consent text must link to a real privacy page. It currently sits next to footer legal links that are href="#".

  This form collects a name, a phone number, and a health-related service. Treat the stored rows as personal health
  information: restrict who can read them, set a retention period, and log access.

  Media Management
  The page uses four images: uploads/backgound.webp as the hero background, uploads/doctor-cutout.png as the hero
  foreground, uploads/team-BJF-74gA.jpg in the about teaser, and uploads/logo.jpeg in the header and footer. Every
  other visual block on the page is a styled placeholder, not a photo.

  The hero background is almost certainly the LCP element. Do not lazy-load it. Preload it. The hero foreground
  image is also above the fold and should not be lazy-loaded either.

  Everything below the hero should be lazy-loaded. Store width and height for every image to stop layout shift.
  Generate WebP/AVIF variants and responsive sizes.

  Note: uploads/ currently holds 18 files but only 4 are referenced. Three pairs are exact duplicates
  (backgound.png, doctor-herosec.png, doctor-herosec.webp). Clean this up during the media migration.

  Performance
  Best fit: SSG or ISR if using Next.js. Homepage content changes occasionally, not per request.

  This page needs the most careful query design on the site because it pulls from five entities at once. Fetch in
  one call: page + SEO + visible sections + section items + selected specialties + selected doctors + selected
  posts + global contact settings + opening hours + media. Do not run one query per section.

  Cache the published payload by locale. Revalidate when any referenced entity changes, not only when the Homepage
  itself is edited, because a doctor or article edit changes what this page shows.

  Keep the specialty filter client-side. It only filters records already loaded.

  Lazy-load the OpenStreetMap iframe. It is at the bottom of the page and blocks nothing above it.

  Security
  Sanitize all teaser copy, CTA URLs, image metadata, and SEO fields. Restrict icons to approved keys; do not allow
  raw SVG from CMS users. The current page stores icons as raw SVG path strings.

  Appointment submissions need the strictest handling on the site: server-side validation, rate limiting, spam
  protection, consent storage, restricted read access, retention policy, and audit logs.

  Do not expose staff notification email addresses in frontend data.

  Current Risks
  The Homepage is the site's most important page and is currently the least production-ready:

  - The appointment form silently discards every submission. A patient sees a success state and no one is contacted.
    This is the single highest-priority defect on the site.
  - Doctor names, qualifications, and NMC registration numbers are placeholders.
  - The testimonial section promises patient experiences and shows none.
  - Two conflicting sources of blog content exist on one page; one of them is dead code.
  - Seven links point to href="#".
  - There is no <title>, meta description, canonical, Open Graph, or structured data.
  - Opening hours are hardcoded in two places on this page and in the header of all seven pages.
  - The public URL contains an encoded space.
  - The footer differs from the other six pages, so the site's global footer is not actually global.

  Best Practice
  Treat the Homepage as a composition layer, not a content owner. Its job is to select, order, and introduce
  records that live in specialties, doctors, and blog_posts. The only content it should truly own is its own
  headings, teaser copy, and the small repeaters in section_items.

  Every time a value on this page is also shown elsewhere, read it from one source. Opening hours, phone, email,
  address, and map coordinates belong to global settings, not to this page.
