About NMC

Healthcare That Feels Personal

NMC brings specialist consultations, diagnostic testing, physiotherapy, women's healthcare, and kidney care together in one convenient location. Our team focuses on listening carefully, explaining clearly, and helping every patient understand the next step in their care.

  • Experienced medical professionals
  • Modern diagnostic facilities
  • Clear and respectful communication
  • Coordinated care under one roof

Our Mission

To make trusted, specialist healthcare easier to reach for families in Butwal — with honest advice, timely tests, and care that treats every patient as a person, not a case number.

Our Vision

To be the clinic our community turns to first — a calm, well-equipped place where consultation, diagnosis, and recovery work together, close to home.

What guides us

The Values Behind Our Care

Simple principles that shape how we treat every patient who walks through our door.

Compassion

We listen first and treat every patient with patience, warmth, and respect.

Trust

Honest advice and clear information — no exaggerated claims, ever.

Excellence

Careful diagnosis and modern facilities to support the right decisions.

Community

Care that stays local, familiar, and accessible to the families we serve.

Our story

How NMC Came Together

  1. 01

    A clear need

    Families in Butwal often travelled between several places for a consultation, a test, and follow-up care. We set out to bring those steps together in one trusted clinic.

  2. 02

    Care under one roof

    We brought specialist consultation, an in-house laboratory, diagnostic imaging, physiotherapy, women's health, and kidney care into a single, welcoming space.

  3. 03

    Growing with our community

    Today we keep listening to our patients and improving — adding services and refining care around what the community around us actually needs.
